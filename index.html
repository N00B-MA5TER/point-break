01 / The Point

Not just about
finding answers.

Point Break tests what you do when your answer stops being enough.

  1. There is no single “correct” answer.What matters is how you understand the problem and justify your choices.
  2. Every problem changes halfway.You solve it, then a twist breaks something you relied on.
  3. You adapt or defend.Change your plan — or prove why it still holds. Both can be right.
  4. Real situations.Inspired by hackathons, projects and decisions our members have actually faced.
  5. No prior knowledge needed.It is about how you think under pressure, not what you already know.

"Point Break is not just about finding an answer.
It is about what you do when the answer stops being enough."

03 / How You Are Judged

Four criteria.

10

Understanding the Problem

Did you grasp what is really being asked — including every constraint?

10

Reasoning & Trade-offs

Can you justify your choice, and say clearly what you gave up for it?

10

Adaptation to Twist

When the situation changes, can you adapt — or defend — your plan with good reasons?

10

Communication

Can you explain your thinking clearly to the room?

Each criterion is scored out of 10. Finding the “intended” answer is not one of them.
